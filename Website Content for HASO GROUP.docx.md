Hero / Intro
Empower. Educate. Transform.
Health Affairs for Youth Initiatives (HAYi) is a youth-focused social impact organization committed to advancing youth health, gender equity, and empowerment. We address the social determinants of health affecting vulnerable, marginalized, and differently-abled youth, ensuring they have the knowledge, services, and opportunities to thrive.
Our Story
HAYi began in 2013 as a creative arts initiative called “Don’t Die Young”, launched by our founder to raise awareness among youth about risky behaviors such as drug abuse, teenage pregnancy, school dropout, and exploitation.
As the campaign grew, it became clear that awareness alone was not enough. Many challenges facing youth are rooted in poverty, limited education, social exclusion, disability discrimination, economic hardship, and structural inequalities , the social determinants of health shaping young lives long before they have choices.
The initiative evolved into Health Affairs Initiative (HAI), transitioning from creative advocacy to structured health education and youth empowerment.
In 2023, while pursuing a Master of Science in Global Health Delivery at the University of Global Health Equity (UGHE), our founder met fellow global health professionals and medical doctors who shared the same commitment to health equity and youth empowerment. Together, they transformed the vision into Health Affairs for Youth Initiatives (HAYi) , a platform dedicated to empowering young people to overcome structural barriers and claim their health, voice, and future.
Mission:
To promote inclusive youth health and well-being through education, advocacy, innovation, and community-driven programs, ensuring that all young people—regardless of gender, background, or ability have equitable access to resources to thrive.
Vision:
A Rwanda where all young people are healthy, empowered, and socially included, with access to quality health services, supportive environments, and economic opportunities that enable them to thrive mentally, physically, and socially.

Why We Exist
Many youth face barriers to health, education, and opportunity — especially those who are vulnerable, marginalized, or living with disabilities. HAYi exists to confront the social determinants of health and transform structural inequalities into opportunities for knowledge, empowerment, and leadership.

2. Programs PAGE 
Overview Text:
HAYi delivers youth-centered programs that address the social determinants of health, empowering vulnerable, marginalized, and differently-abled youth to thrive. Each program combines evidence-based approaches, community engagement, and innovative youth-led solutions.
SRHR, HIV & GBV Prevention & Safe Spaces
Program Overview
HAYi’s SRHR, HIV & GBV Prevention & Safe Spaces program empowers youth to make informed decisions about their sexual and reproductive health, protect themselves from HIV and other STIs, and prevent gender-based violence. By addressing the social determinants of health, the program ensures vulnerable, marginalized, and differently-abled youth have access to knowledge, services, and safe spaces to thrive physically, emotionally, and socially.
Key Projects
Menya, Wirinde, Ubeho
A youth-focused initiative that raises awareness on sexual and reproductive health, consent, healthy relationships, and gender equality. Students participate in interactive workshops, peer-led sessions, and campaigns to reduce risky behaviors and promote informed decision-making.
UMURINZI
A youth advocates for safe spaces in schools which trains and empowers students to become advocates for safe, inclusive, and supportive school environments. UMURINZI equips youth to identify risks, respond to peer challenges, and champion equality and protection within their communities.
Mental Health & Wellbeing Program
Program Overview
HAYi’s Mental Health & Wellbeing program empowers youth and school staff to build resilience, manage stress, and create supportive environments. By addressing the social determinants of health, the program ensures that vulnerable, marginalized, and differently-abled youth have access to mental health knowledge, guidance, and safe spaces.
Key Projects
MindUp
A youth-centered initiative that provides peer support, stress management workshops, and digital campaigns to promote emotional wellbeing. MindUp helps students develop coping skills, confidence, and resilience.
Mentora
A professional development program for teachers and school staff, Mentora equips educators with tools to support students’ mental health, foster safe and inclusive school environments, and guide youth toward positive outcomes.
Healthy Lifestyle, Nutrition & WASH
Program Overview
HAYi’s Healthy Lifestyle, Nutrition & WASH program empowers youth to adopt balanced diets, stay active, practice good hygiene, and safely consume water. By addressing the social determinants of health, the program ensures vulnerable, marginalized, and differently-abled youth have access to the knowledge, habits, and safe practices they need to thrive.
Key Projects
HealthEmpower
A youth-focused initiative promoting physical activity, sports, and nutrition education. HealthEmpower engages students through interactive workshops, fitness sessions, and campaigns to build healthy lifestyles and prevent non-communicable diseases.
NutriChamp
Raises awareness on nutrition, reduces unhealthy eating practices, and fosters food literacy. NutriChamp provides practical guidance, workshops, and mentoring for students and school staff to make informed dietary choices.
MajiLife
A WASH-focused project promoting safe water consumption, hygiene, and sanitation practices. MajiLife encourages students to drink clean water, use proper sanitation facilities, and adopt hygiene habits through interactive activities, campaigns, and the distribution of water bottles to support healthy hydration.
Leadership, Advocacy & Policy Engagement
Program Overview
HAYi’s Leadership, Advocacy & Policy Engagement program empowers youth to develop leadership skills, influence health and social policies, and drive positive change in their communities. By addressing the social determinants of health, the program ensures vulnerable, marginalized, and differently-abled youth have opportunities to participate, learn, and amplify their voices.
Key Projects
NextWave
A media-driven series (podcast and YouTube talks) showcasing youth leaders, health advocates, and community change-makers. NextWave inspires and equips young people to take action, share ideas, and lead initiatives that promote health, gender equity, and social impact.
Policy Spotlight
A digital series that explores youth engagement in health and social policies. Policy Spotlight features success stories, interviews with experts, and actionable guidance for youth to contribute to advocacy, leadership, and community initiatives.

Career Development & Socio-Economic Empowerment
Program Overview:
HAYi’s Career Development & Socio-Economic Empowerment program equips youth with skills, mentorship, and economic opportunities to build sustainable futures. It focuses on empowering vulnerable, marginalized, and differently-abled youth by addressing key social determinants of health.
Project Overview 
LevelUp
LevelUp is a flexible, youth-centered initiative that combines workshops, mentorship, hands-on projects, digital learning, career support, and financial literacy support to help young people develop career skills, leadership, and entrepreneurial opportunities.
